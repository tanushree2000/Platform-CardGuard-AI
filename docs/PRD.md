# PRD — CardGuard AI
**Author:** Tanushree Poojary  
**Role:** AI Product Manager

## Problem
Unrecognized-transaction support spans transaction lookup, policy explanation, dispute intake, account protection and Fraud Operations. A chatbot only explains; a fully autonomous agent creates unacceptable control risk.

## Product decision
I designed a bounded agent. **Reasoning is not authorization.**

AI may classify, retrieve, summarize, explain and recommend. Deterministic product policy controls tools, account state, approvals, persistence and escalation.

## MVP
Transaction retrieval; intent routing; governed policy retrieval; synthetic dispute creation; explicit approval before card lock; escalation; prompt-injection protection; PII redaction; audit trail; customer UI; Fraud Ops console; regression evals.

## North Star
**Safe Resolutions per 1,000 Eligible Agent Sessions.**

## Hard guardrail
Unauthorized consequential actions: **0**.

## Rollout
Prototype → offline/adversarial eval → shadow mode → specialist-assisted → limited pilot → evidence-based autonomy expansion.

All data and account actions are synthetic.
